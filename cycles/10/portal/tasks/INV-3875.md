## Authority and freshness

INV-3875 was created as an independent Cycle 10 foundation after clarification showed that the
Metrics tabs could not safely consume the Collector from the browser. Linear and the final merged
repositories remain canonical for current status and implementation.

## Durable clarification

Portal API is the tenant-safe BFF between a permissioned Portal session and the Apex Gateway /
Collector metrics route. It derives the active organization server-side, resolves an explicit
organization-to-Collector mapping, generates 15 UTC calendar-month buckets, fans out with bounded
concurrency, normalizes partial and total failures, and publishes an API-owned contract consumed by
Portal Web.

## Decisions preserved

- The browser cannot choose organization ID, Collector client ID, `as_org`, metric families, or
  date window.
- Portal API enforces `portal.client.metrics.view` for the active organization before upstream work.
- The mapping is explicit, unique, server-owned, and provisioned idempotently per environment. A
  missing mapping fails closed.
- Collector access uses a dedicated service credential boundary; secrets never enter the database,
  browser response, logs, or this record.
- The BFF produces exactly 15 ordered UTC buckets, including the partial current month.
- Fan-out concurrency is bounded at three, buckets are reordered deterministically, and no Portal
  API cache is added in v1.
- Canonical values, methodology, quality, cache, staleness, and age are preserved through an
  allow-listed response. Missing or failed values never become zero.
- At least one usable value yields a partial response; no usable values yield a typed total error.
- The shared Web layer owns loading, refresh, complete, partial, stale, unavailable, and total-error
  state without implementing either final tab UI.
- The Portal API ADR records the identity, authority, mapping, credential, and rollout boundaries.

## Boundaries and dependencies

- Apex Gateway and the Collector remain owners of authentication at their boundary, organization
  isolation, and metric methodology.
- INV-3832 supplies the Portal permission contract and navigation gate.
- INV-3833 and INV-3834 consume the foundation for their respective tabs.
- Environment connectivity, secret provisioning, mappings, and authorized smoke are explicit
  rollout conditions; they are not embedded in source code or silently assumed from a local test.
