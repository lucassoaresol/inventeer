## Durable clarification

INV-3828 establishes a dedicated Ticket domain rather than adapting the governed Request domain.
The Portal is the only creation channel in v1. A conversational intake produces a preview, but the
Ticket exists only after explicit human confirmation.

The coherent delivery boundary is cross-repository: Portal API owns Ticket persistence, public
contracts, authorization, tenant derivation, idempotent confirmation, attachments, audit events,
and the minimum list/detail read contract; Portal Web owns the conversation, preview, upload,
confirmation, recovery states, and consumption of the published contract.

## Decisions preserved

- `Ticket` is not a Request alias, status, or IDS workflow variant.
- `Source` is server-owned and resolves to `Portal` in v1.
- The CareWebs reference is required, format-validated, and persisted without an external CareWebs
  lookup in v1.
- Requesting organization, serving organization, requester, and source are derived or validated by
  the backend rather than trusted from the browser.
- Confirmation is the idempotent creation boundary; retry and double-submit must not duplicate a
  Ticket.
- Ticket attachments require a dedicated tenant-safe storage boundary. The same secure capability
  should be reusable for evidence attached to later requester responses.
- INV-3828 publishes the write path plus the minimum tenant-safe read contract. INV-3830 consumes
  that contract for the full home experience.
- `Waiting on you` and `Reopened` are canonical server-owned states for downstream lifecycle work.

## Boundaries and dependencies

- Slack, Cowork, Linear, and CareWebs integrations are not creation channels in v1.
- Ticket behavior must not expose internal Linear items, Labs work, or IDS artifacts.
- Full visual proof that a newly confirmed Ticket appears in Tickets Home depends on INV-3830.
- Lifecycle actions, public timeline, deep-link behavior, and notification emission belong to
  downstream contracts, principally INV-3831 and INV-3847.
