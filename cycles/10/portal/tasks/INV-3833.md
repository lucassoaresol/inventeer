## Authority and freshness

Linear remains canonical for the issue lifecycle. The Collector and Apex Gateway remain canonical
for metric methodology; Portal API owns the tenant-safe delivery contract; Portal Web owns the
presentation. Revalidate those sources before evolving the tab.

## Durable clarification

The Technical tab renders seven DORA and reliability cards from a fixed rolling 15-month UTC series.
It exposes the Collector's canonical values and methodology with minimal presentation adaptation,
preserving missing, partial, stale, and current-partial data instead of inventing zeros.

## Decisions preserved

- The v1 window is fixed at 15 UTC calendar-month buckets; the current month is partial.
- The tab uses the canonical `dora`, `incident`, and `pr_review_time` families without redefining
  metric methodology in Portal.
- All/Bugs/Non-bugs selectors are absent in v1 because the upstream contract did not support that
  segmentation.
- Seven cards are grouped under Speed, Reliability, and Quality and share chart, table, formatter,
  accessibility, and server-state seams.
- Every chart has a deterministic textual trend and an accessible monthly-value alternative.
- Partial values remain renderable with an aggregate warning; only total failure replaces the
  surface with an error state.
- Tenant identity, organization mapping, fan-out, authorization, and normalization belong to the
  shared foundation in INV-3875, not to browser code.
