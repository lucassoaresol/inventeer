## Durable clarification

Delivery Flow v1 is the smallest truthful surface supported by the existing `flow` family. It
publishes and renders Delivered per week, approximate Work in progress, and Bug Ratio (All) over the
same tenant-safe 15-month series and shared presentation seams as Technical Metrics.

## Decisions preserved

- `flow.flow_velocity_per_week` drives Delivered per week.
- `flow.flow_distribution_bug_pct` drives Bug Ratio (All).
- `flow.flow_load_avg` is an approximation based on Little's Law, not a direct count of open work;
  the approximation must be disclosed in visible copy.
- Issue Lead Time, Rework rate, Aging work, and unsupported selectors are absent from v1 rather than
  shown with fabricated or placeholder values.
- Portal API owns `GET /api/metrics/delivery-flow`, its allow-listed response contract, active-org
  resolution, and preservation of zero versus unavailable data.
- Portal Web fetches the active tab, uses a defensively parsed tab address, reuses shared charts and
  tables, and keeps locale parity.
- A missing or failed upstream value remains `null`/unavailable; it never becomes zero.
