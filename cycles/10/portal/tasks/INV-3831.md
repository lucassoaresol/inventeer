## Durable clarification

Ticket Detail is a route-backed overlay over Tickets Home. It exposes a requester-visible timeline
and only those actions allowed by the backend for the current actor and state. It is not an audit-log
viewer, a Linear projection, or a frontend-owned state machine.

## Decisions preserved

- The canonical detail address is `/app/tickets/:ticketId`, including when rendered as a sheet.
- Direct open, refresh, back/forward, close, and return to the filtered list are part of the route
  contract rather than incidental component state.
- The public timeline is typed, ordered by server-owned sequence/revision, and filtered to
  requester-visible content.
- `Waiting on you` and `Reopened` are persisted lifecycle states.
- The requester who created the Ticket can reopen once from `Resolved` or `Declined`; the quota
  remains visible and concurrency must not consume it twice.
- A decline requires a requester-visible reason.
- `Cancel ticket` and evidence attachments on requester responses are part of the v1 contract.
- The API publishes allowed actions; hiding or showing a button in the Web does not grant authority.
- Stale paginated timeline responses must not overwrite newer revisions.
